In this seminar, student teachers are prepared for their final exams in theoretical physics revising the essentials of mechanics, electrodynamics, quantum theory, and thermodynamics / statistical physics. Each three-week section of the course is dedicated to one of the major fields of theoretical physics relevant in school contexts. Students discuss important concepts and solve problems that are prototypical for the oral or written state examination in Thuringia.
